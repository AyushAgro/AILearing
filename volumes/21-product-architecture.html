From Product Problem to AI Architecture

An AI architecture that does not start from a product problem is a box diagram looking for a budget. This chapter turns a problem statement into requirements, SLOs, a risk register and ADRs that later chapters can implement.

The Engineering Problem

A stakeholder asks for "a ChatGPT for our support tickets". Engineering sketches a chatbot. Six months later the system cannot meet the refund SLA, leaks one tenant's policy into another's answer, and the bill grows linearly with ticket volume. The failure was not the model. It was the missing product-to-architecture translation.

Possible approaches

  • Demo-first. Prompt a frontier API until a demo impresses. Fast; silent on SLOs, tenancy and cost.
  • Platform-first. Build every box in the reference HLD before a single user journey works. Slow; often over-built for the first use case.
  • Problem → contract → architecture. Write who the user is, what success and failure look like, which risks are unacceptable, then choose only the boxes that enforce those contracts. This volume's default.

Key Idea
  • Write the SLO before the model name. A model is a means; the SLO is the contract.
  • Separate must never (cross-tenant leak, unapproved refund) from should usually (helpful tone). Architecture enforces the first; evaluation tracks the second.

Risk Register for AI Products

  • Model risk. Hallucination, refusal gaps, capability cliffs on long context.
  • Data risk. Training or retrieval leakage; retention past policy; poisoned connectors.
  • Agency risk. Tool side effects the harness cannot roll back.
  • Cost risk. Unbounded agent loops; cache miss storms; frontier-only routing.
  • Ops risk. Provider outage without a degraded mode; silent quality drift.

Each risk needs an owner, a residual rating and a control that lives on a trust boundary — not only in a system prompt.

Architecture Decision Records

ADRs capture decisions that are expensive to reverse: where tenancy is enforced, whether the product is a workflow or an agent, which cache layers exist, and what "degraded" means to the user. Volume 1 introduced the habit; here it becomes a first-class artefact in services/adr.py.

# ADR-0007: Tenant ACL enforced in retrieval planner
Status: Accepted · 2026-09-29

## Context
Cross-tenant leakage was the highest residual risk on the enterprise assistant.

## Decision
Filter by tenant_id and ACL before ranking; never rely on the prompt to honour tenancy.

## Consequences
- Extra join on every retrieval
- Prompts stay tenant-agnostic
- Eval must include cross-tenant probes

Write ADR-0001 for your product: workflow versus agent for the first journey. List three consequences you are accepting. If you cannot name a consequence, you do not yet have a decision.

Data Architecture for AI Products

Models are ephemeral. The durable product is the data plane: conversations, feedback, vectors, events and retention rules. This chapter designs that plane so privacy and analytics do not fight each other.

The Engineering Problem

Product wants "remember this user". Legal wants a 30-day retention clock. Analytics wants six months of prompts for quality work. Security wants no prompts in the same lake as billing identifiers. Without an explicit data architecture, one Postgres table absorbs all four demands and satisfies none.

Retention and Privacy

  • Purpose limitation. Prompts used for debugging are not automatically licensed for training. Separate consents and pipelines.
  • Redaction at write. Store content_ref to a vault that can scrub PII; events keep token counts and hashes, not free text, when policy requires.
  • Right to erase. Deleting a conversation must tombstone vector chunks derived from user-uploaded files for that tenant.
  • Access control. Tenant isolation is a query filter, not a prompt instruction (Chapter 8).
Key Idea
  • Design the delete path on the same day as the write path.
  • Feedback that trains routers (Volume 18) should be preference-shaped and tenant-scoped — the same discipline as conversation storage.

Event Log Shape

Volume 22 will scrape these events into metrics. If the field is missing here, FinOps invents a spreadsheet later.

{"cache_hit": false, "error_class": null, "input_tokens": 120, "latency_ms": 210.5, "model": "mid-open", "name": "model.chat", "output_tokens": 40, "request_id": "req1", "route_reason": "policy_table", "session_id": "s1", "status": "ok", "tenant_id": "acme"}

Analytics Without Becoming a Second Product

  • Slice keys. Intent, model tier, cache hit, tenant tier, guard code — enough to debug quality and cost without storing raw prompts in the warehouse by default.
  • Join discipline. Feedback joins on turn_id; never re-embed free text from analytics copies.
  • Sampling. Online judges (Volume 10) read from the event stream with a rate limit, not from a nightly dump of every prompt.

For your product, write the retention row for each store in the table above. If legal and product disagree, that disagreement is an ADR, not a Slack thread.

Case Study: General Chat Assistants

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are the public face of general assistants. This chapter separates what their organisations have published from what engineers reasonably infer when designing similar products.

The Product Problem

A consumer or knowledge worker wants a conversational interface over a general model: ask, refine, optionally browse or use tools, and continue across sessions. Latency must feel interactive; safety and policy must hold at internet scale.

Documented Versus Inferred

Documented (public artefacts)

  • Documented OpenAI's GPT-4 Technical Report and System Card (2023) describe capability evaluations, safety evaluations and mitigation themes for GPT-4-class models.
  • Documented The Gemini Team technical report (2023) describes Gemini as multimodal natively and reports benchmark results across modalities.
  • Documented Anthropic publishes model cards and product documentation describing Constitutional AI themes, tool use and products such as Projects and computer-use previews as they ship.
  • Documented All three providers document streaming token APIs, system/developer messages and (with varying APIs) tool or function calling.

Inferred (architecture hypotheses)

  • Inferred A multi-tier model fleet with routing by capability, load and cost — consistent with public "Fast" / default / advanced product tiers, but internal router details are not fully specified in system cards.
  • Inferred Layered input/output classifiers and policy engines in front of and behind the generative model — suggested by system-card mitigation language, not a published service graph.
  • Inferred Conversation stores with retention controls, abuse-review pipelines and prompt-cache / KV reuse for long system prompts — standard for the product class; exact schemas are proprietary.
  • Inferred Memory features (saved facts, project files) sit beside the context builder with retrieval and ACL, not only as "more tokens in the window".
Key Idea
  • Copying a marketing screenshot is not architecture. Copying a documented API contract is.
  • When you sketch "how ChatGPT works internally", tag every box. Untagged boxes become false confidence in design review.

Case Study: Code Completion

Inline code completion is an inference product under a hard latency budget. GitHub Copilot is the reference product class; the engineering lessons are FIM-shaped context, telemetry and careful productivity measurement.

The Product Problem

As the developer types, the editor needs a suggestion within roughly a hundred milliseconds of perceived delay. Context includes the current file, nearby files and the cursor position. Wrong suggestions are cheap to dismiss; slow suggestions are ignored.

Documented

  • Documented Chen et al., 2021 (Codex) describe large language models fine-tuned for code and evaluate on HumanEval-style benchmarks.
  • Documented Ziegler et al., 2022 report observational measurements of Copilot users (acceptance rates and related productivity signals) in a peer-reviewed study context.
  • Documented Peng et al., 2023 run a controlled experiment on Copilot and report substantial task-time reductions for a HTTP-server implementation task in their study setting.
  • Documented Public Copilot / model docs describe fill-in-the-middle (FIM) style completion APIs used by coding models (prefix and suffix around the cursor).

Inferred

  • Inferred Speculative local / small-model drafting with optional cloud verification to hit interactive latency — common pattern; not a published Copilot wiring diagram.
  • Inferred Aggressive caching of prefix computations and editor-side debounce / cancellation when the cursor moves.
  • Inferred Telemetry pipelines that log accept / dismiss / edit-after-accept for offline evaluation — consistent with published measurement papers, exact schemas unknown.

Case Study: Coding Agents

Coding agents plan, edit, run tests and iterate. The product is the harness: permissions, sandbox, budgets and evaluation on repositories — not a single completion call.

The Product Problem

A user delegates a software task ("fix the flaky test", "implement the endpoint"). The system must read the repo, edit files, run commands and stop when done or when the budget expires — without exfiltrating secrets or destroying the machine.

Documented

  • Documented Yang et al., 2024 (SWE-agent) describe an agent–computer interface for software engineering and evaluate on SWE-bench.
  • Documented SWE-bench (Jimenez et al., 2024 and follow-ons) provides real GitHub issue–PR pairs as an evaluation harness for coding agents.
  • Documented Public docs for products such as Claude Code describe permission prompts before commands and edits (Volume 16).
  • Documented The Model Context Protocol (Anthropic, 2024) standardises tool list/call boundaries over JSON-RPC.

Inferred

  • Inferred Production isolation may use containers, microVMs or remote executors depending on vendor and tier — treat blog specifics as version-bound claims.
  • Inferred Long jobs use durable session logs (Volume 16 lifecycle) with resume after disconnect.
  • Inferred Patch ranking and test-gated commit loops sit outside the base model as harness policy.

  • SWE-bench success without a permission model is a research demo, not a product.

Case Study: Enterprise Assistants

Enterprise assistants fail first on permissions, connectors and tenancy — not on eloquence. ACL-aware RAG is the load-bearing design.

The Product Problem

Employees ask questions over SharePoint, tickets and wikis. Answers must respect document ACLs, tenant isolation and compliance logging. A fluent wrong-tenant answer is a security incident.

Documented

  • Documented Major cloud assistants document connectors to enterprise content sources and admin controls for enabling them (product docs; exact ACL propagation differs by vendor).
  • Documented RAG literature and Volume 14 treat retrieved documents as untrusted text that still requires access control at query time.

  • Inferred A connector framework with incremental sync, tombstones and per-document ACL fields in the index.
  • Inferred Query planner applies tenant_id and user principal filters before similarity ranking (ADR-0007 in Chapter 1).

Case Study: Customer Support Agents

Support is where workflows usually beat open-ended agents — until the ticket needs investigation. Escalation, policy compliance and deflection metrics define success.

The Product Problem

Customers ask about orders, refunds and outages. Many tickets are repetitive; some need tools; a few need a human. The business cares about deflection, handle time, CSAT and policy breaches — not perplexity.

Documented

  • Documented Brynjolfsson et al., 2023 study generative AI in customer support and report productivity gains for agents, especially less-experienced workers, in their setting.
  • Documented Anthropic's Building effective agents (2024) argues for starting with workflows and adding agency only when needed — directly applicable to support.
  • Documented Volume 15's workflow-versus-agent framing and Volume 18's escalation/abstention utilities are the engineering counterparts.

Inferred

  • Inferred A classifier or rules router sends FAQ to a workflow, complex tickets to a bounded agent, and high-risk intents to humans.
  • Inferred Refund tools require harness approval above a threshold; transcripts feed quality review.
  • Inferred Deflection is measured as resolved-without-human among tickets that would have entered the queue — not as "model replied".

Cost Architecture

Unit economics turn architecture choices into a bill. Caching, routing and capacity planning are cost controls with quality side constraints — not afterthoughts for finance.

The Engineering Problem

Traffic grows 3×. The model bill grows 3×. Leadership asks why routing and caches from Volumes 12 and 18 are not visible in the invoice. Nobody owned conversation-level unit economics.

Design Review Method

A design review is a structured argument that quality, safety, cost and operability hold — not a slide aesthetic contest. This chapter gives the checklist.

The Engineering Problem

Two teams present HLDs. Both have boxes labelled "guardrails" and "eval". Only one can answer: where is tenancy enforced, what is the degraded mode, and which golden tests gate launch? Review without a checklist rewards confidence.

Two pieces of evidence shape the checklist. Parnin et al. (2023) interviewed engineers building product copilots and found the same pain points across teams: prompt fragility, no testing methodology for non-deterministic output, and evaluation built after launch rather than before. Shankar et al. (2024) showed that LLM graders need their own validation: reviewers' criteria shift as they read outputs (criteria drift), so evaluator assertions must be aligned with human grades on a sample and re-checked as the product changes. A design review therefore asks not only "is there an eval?" but "who validated the evaluator, and when?"

Important Architecture

Failure Modes

  • HLD boxes without ADRs or owners.
  • Inferred vendor internals presented as documented fact.
  • Cache keys missing tenant or policy version.
  • Retries on non-idempotent tool calls.
  • RAG without ACL filter-before-rank.
  • Open-ended support agents where a workflow suffices.
  • Token bills without \$/successful-task.
  • No delete path for vectors when conversations are erased.
  • Design review that never asks for degraded mode or traces.

Evaluation

  • Golden journeys per product class (chat, completion latency, SWE-bench-style for coding agents, cross-tenant probes for enterprise, citation checks for research, deflection+policy for support).
  • Online: sample traces into judges; alert on policy denials and cost anomalies.
  • Contract tests on services/ invariants above in CI.

RESEARCH PAPER Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction Amershi et al., 2019 · CHI
Problem
AI products need consistent interaction principles beyond model accuracy.
Previous limitation
Ad hoc UX advice without a validated guideline set.
Approach
Eighteen guidelines covering timing, control, mental models and recovery.
Contribution
A shared checklist enterprise and consumer assistants still reuse.
Practical impact
Chapter 12 review asks for recovery paths and appropriate confidence.
Limitations
Guidelines are not a substitute for threat models or SLOs.
RESEARCH PAPER Productivity Assessment of Neural Code Completion Ziegler et al., 2022 · MAPS / Copilot study
Problem
Do neural completions change real developer behaviour?
Previous limitation
Benchmark pass rates without in-editor telemetry.
Approach
Observational measurements of acceptance and related signals in Copilot use.
Contribution
Grounded productivity proxies for the completion product class.
Practical impact
Chapter 6's insistence on telemetry and careful citation of study design.
Limitations
Observational — complements, does not replace, controlled experiments.
RESEARCH PAPER The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot Peng et al., 2023
Problem
Causal evidence for Copilot's effect on task completion time.
Previous limitation
Anecdotes and observational acceptance rates alone.
Approach
Controlled experiment on a standardised programming task.
Contribution
Large reported speed-up in their experimental setting.
Practical impact
Chapter 6 measurement discipline; do not over-generalise beyond the task.
Limitations
One task family; industry tasks vary in review and compliance overhead.
RESEARCH PAPER SWE-agent: Agent-Computer Interfaces Enable Automated Software Engineering Yang et al., 2024
